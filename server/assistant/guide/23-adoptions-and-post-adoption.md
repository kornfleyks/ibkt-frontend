# Adoptions and post-adoption follow-up

## The Adoptions page

The "Adoptions" page lists the applications that are in the stage Approved Application. Open it from "Adoptions" in the sidebar. Admins and Volunteers can see the page.

An Admin sees every adoption. A Volunteer sees only the adoptions where that Volunteer is the Case Owner. An Admin can turn on the "My cases" switch to see only their own.

Each adoption is a card with the applicant name, the city and country, the stage, the Case Owner and the linked cat. A card with no linked cat says "No cat linked". Use the "Search adoptions" box to filter by name, email, phone, cat or owner. Click a card to open the adoption page.

When no adoption matches, the page says "No adoptions yet." or "No adoptions match" followed by your search.

An application leaves this page when its stage is no longer Approved Application, for example when it becomes Completed.

## The adoption page

The adoption page shows the details of an approved application. Volunteers who are the Case Owner can read it. Only Admins see the "Edit" button.

The page has these cards.

- "Applicant Information": name, email, phone, country, city, address, application ID and created date.
- "Case Information": Case Owner, Assigned Volunteer, "Priority", "Adoption Stage", "Case Health" and the linked cat.
- "Application Details": the applicant's answers.
- "Calls", "Video" and "References": the screening results.
- "Matching & Decision": "Match Confidence", "Team Decision" and "Decision Notes".
- "Contract" and "Payment": the contract statuses and the payment status.
- "AI Review": the accepted AI review values.
- "Internal Notes".

An Admin clicks "Edit" to change "Priority", "Adoption Stage", "Case Health", "Team Decision", "Decision Notes" and "Internal Notes". Click "Save Changes" to keep the changes or "Cancel" to leave them. The Case Owner and the Assigned Volunteer are changed on the application page, not here.

From Approved Application the stage can be changed to Completed Application, Rejected Application or Archived Application. It cannot be set back to New Application or Active Application.

The full set of tabs, such as "Contracts", "Payments", "Travel" and "Post Adoption", is on the application page. The adoption page does not have these tabs. Approved applications are not listed on the "Active Applications" page, so open the application page from a link, such as the linked application on a task.

## Post-adoption follow-up

Post-adoption follow-up checks on the cat and the adopter after the cat has gone home. It lives on the "Post Adoption" tab of the application page. Admins and the Case Owner of the application can see the tab. Other users see a message that the details are only visible to Admins and the Case Owner.

### Starting post-adoption

Before a record exists, the tab asks for an "Adoption date" (required) and an "Arrival date (optional)". Click "Start post-adoption". Oriel links the record to the application and the cat and schedules nine check-ins counted from the arrival date. When there is no arrival date, it counts from the adoption date.

### The check-ins

The nine check-ins are "24 hours", "72 hours", "Week 1", "Week 2", "Week 3", "Week 4", "3 months", "6 months" and "1 year".

- Each check-in shows a due date. Click the due date to change it.
- Each check-in has a status: Not Due, Sent, Received, Concern or No Response.
- Choose "Sent" when you have contacted the adopter. Choose "Received" when the adopter has replied. Oriel does not contact the adopter. Staff contact the adopter outside Oriel and record it here.
- A check-in that is past its due date and has no reply shows "Overdue".
- Changing the arrival date moves only the check-ins that are still Not Due.
- The "All check-ins complete" label shows when every check-in is done. Otherwise it reads "Check-ins in progress".

### Reminders and chasing

Oriel reminds staff, and does not message the adopter. Reminders arrive as notifications that link to the "Post Adoption" tab.

- Oriel sends a reminder when a check-in is due and not yet sent, and again every 3 days, up to 3 reminders. If it is still not sent after that, the owner, the Case Owner and the Admins are told that it is being missed.
- Marking a check-in "Sent" counts as attempt 1. A check-in waiting for a reply shows "Attempt 1 of 3".
- After 3 days with no reply, Oriel reminds the owner to chase again. Click "Chased again" each time you contact the adopter. That makes it the next attempt.
- After the third attempt, when there is still no reply 3 days later, the check-in becomes No Response and "Stop chasing" is switched on. Escalation Required becomes Yes (it stays Urgent if it was Urgent). A line is added to the escalation notes, and the owner, the Case Owner and the Admins are told.
- Marking a check-in "Received", "Concern", "No Response" or "Not Due" ends its chasing. Marking it "Sent" again starts a new count at attempt 1, which is also how to resume after chasing stopped.
- The "Stop chasing" switch stops all reminders for that adoption.

Reminders go to the "Owner" of the post-adoption record. When there is no owner, they go to the Case Owner of the application and all Admins.

### The Summary card

The "Summary" card shows chips for the post-adoption status, "Escalation" and the "AI concern flag". It also shows the next check-in and its due date. These fields can be edited.

- "Status": Not Started, Active, Waiting for Response, Concern, Completed or Archived. Reminders run only while the status is Active, Waiting for Response or Concern.
- "Owner": the person responsible for the follow-up.
- "Adoption date", "Arrival date" and "Closed date".
- Read-only values: "Last check-in sent", "Last response" and "Chase count".

### Updates and concerns

The "Updates and concerns" card has "Escalation required" (Yes, No or Urgent), "Escalation notes", "General update", "Health update", "Behaviour update" and "Internal notes". Setting "Escalation required" to Urgent notifies the Case Owner and all Admins.

### Photos and videos

The "Photos and videos" card lists photos and videos from the adopter. Click "Upload" to add files. Files can be deleted after a confirmation.

### AI check-in review

The "AI check-in review" card shows an AI "Concern flag" (Green, Yellow or Red), a "Check-in summary" and a "Suggested next action". Before the AI has produced anything, each shows "Not generated yet". The card says that the text is generated by AI and must be checked against the updates before acting on it. This card is read-only. No button on the tab starts the AI check-in review.

### Testimonial requests

The "Testimonial" card records a request for a testimonial from the adopter. Staff ask the adopter themselves, outside Oriel. The section is available at any time. The status can be Not requested, Requested, Received, Declined or No response.

- "Mark as requested" records that you asked. It counts as attempt 1. After Declined or No response, the same button is named "Request again".
- "Asked again" adds one more request to a Requested testimonial, up to 3.
- "Record testimonial" saves the testimonial text and a tick box "The adopter agreed that it may be published". Then click "Save testimonial". The status becomes Received.
- "Declined" records that the adopter does not want to give one.
- "Edit" changes the text or the permission of a Received testimonial.
- "Reset" removes the request and the text. For a Received testimonial, Oriel asks for confirmation first.
- Oriel reminds the owner 3 days after a request to ask again. After 3 requests with no reply, the status becomes No response and the owner is told.
- The permission to publish is only recorded. Oriel does not publish a testimonial.
