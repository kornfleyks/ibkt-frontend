# Forms page and public forms

## What the Forms page is for

The "Forms" page lists the forms that staff can send to other people. Open it from "Forms" in the sidebar. Admins and Volunteers can see it. The subtitle reads "Links to the forms you can send to people."

Each form is shown as a card with its title, a short description and its full link. Each card has two buttons.

- "Copy link" copies the link, so you can paste it into an email or message. A green message confirms that the link was copied.
- "Open" opens the form in a new browser tab, so you can see what the person will see.

## The forms on the page

- "Rescuer Application": a new rescuer applies to work with the charity. It opens only from the sign-up link that a rescuer receives after signing the Rescuer Agreement, so the plain address does not open the form. The application is approved automatically and the rescuer is signed in. An Admin reviews it only if the account could not be created.
- "Foster Application": a new foster applies with the Foster Compliance and Safeguarding Form. An Admin reviews it, then approves or rejects it.
- "Pre-Adoption Form": an applicant tells us about themselves and the cat they are interested in. It creates a new application.
- "Adoption Form and References": the second step for applicants outside the United Arab Emirates, with three personal references. It is added to the applicant's application.
- "UAE Adoption Form & Agreement": the second step for applicants in the United Arab Emirates, including the signed adoption agreement. It is added to the applicant's application.
- "Reference Check": a referee answers questions about an applicant. It is added to the applicant's application, under "References".

## What the person filling in a form sees

Public forms need no sign-in. The person opens the link and answers the questions in steps, using "Next" and "Back". Required questions must be answered before they can continue. Some forms allow file uploads, such as photos or identity documents. After sending, the person sees a "Thank You" page.

Signatures on these forms are a typed full name, not a drawn signature.

## What happens after someone submits a form

- Pre-Adoption Form: a new application is created in the stage New Application. It appears on the "New Applications" tab of the "Active Applications" page.
- Adoption Form and References, and UAE Adoption Form & Agreement: the answers are added to the applicant's application. The application then moves from New Application to Active Application. When the UAE agreement arrives signed, "Signed Contract Received" is set to Yes. The applicant's identity documents are stored with the application.
- Reference Check: the referee answers appear on the "References" tab of the application. The first answer sets "References submitted" to Yes.
- Oriel finds the right application by the application link, then by email address, then by name. When no application matches, the submission is kept as unmatched and every active Admin receives a notification. The person who filled in the form still sees the normal thank-you message.

## Sending the second adoption form

For an applicant in the United Arab Emirates, staff send the second form with the "Email References" button on the application page. That button creates a link with the applicant details already filled in. See the guide section about the application page. The links on the "Forms" page are plain links without pre-filled details.
