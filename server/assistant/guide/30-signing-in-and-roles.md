# Signing In, Roles and Finding Your Way Around Oriel

## Who uses Oriel

Oriel has three staff roles: Super Admin, Admin and Volunteer. There are also two outside roles, Rescue Partner and Foster. Outside roles do not use the staff app. They have their own small portals, which are described in the Rescue Partners and Fosters guides.

## Signing in

1. Open the Oriel sign-in page, called "Sign In".
2. Type your email address in "Email" and your password in "Password".
3. Press "Sign In". The button stays disabled until both boxes are filled in.

After a successful sign-in, Oriel opens the Dashboard.

If the sign-in fails, a red message appears above the button. If your session ended because an Admin suspended or blocked your account, a yellow message says why. Ask an Admin to reactivate or unblock the account.

Repeated wrong passwords lock the account for a while. An Admin can set a new password for you on the Users page (see the Users guide).

Rescue partners and fosters do not use this page. A rescue partner signs in on "Rescuer Sign In" and a foster signs in on "Foster Sign In". If someone uses the wrong sign-in page, Oriel tells them which page to use.

## Creating an account

A new volunteer can create an account from the sign-in page.

1. Press "Create an account".
2. Fill in "First Name", "Last Name", "Email", "Password" (at least 8 characters), "Confirm Password" and "Invite Code". Every box is required, and you need the invite code from IBKT.
3. Press "Create Account".

The new account has the status Pending and the role Volunteer. It cannot sign in until an Admin approves it on the Users page. Approving it gives the person Volunteer access to the staff app, so an Admin should check who the person is first.

## Forgotten password

1. On the sign-in page, press "Forgot password?".
2. On the "Reset Password" page, type your email and press "Send Reset Link".
3. Open the email and press the link. The link works for 15 minutes.
4. On the "Choose a New Password" page, type "New Password" and "Confirm New Password" (at least 8 characters) and press "Set New Password".
5. Oriel returns you to the sign-in page. Sign in with the new password.

Oriel shows the same message whether or not the email belongs to an account, so the page does not reveal who has an account. Only active accounts receive the email. Oriel sends at most one reset email every 15 minutes, so if the email does not arrive, check the spam folder and wait 15 minutes before asking for another. Resetting a password signs the account out on every device.

If the reset link opens a page that says the link is incomplete, press "request a new one" and start again.

## My Account page

Open the round avatar at the top right of any page and choose "Profile" or "Settings". Both open the "My Account" page, which has two tabs, "Profile" and "Settings". Every signed-in staff member can use this page.

### Profile tab

- The "Profile" card shows your name, phone, email, role, account status and latest sign-in. Press "Edit profile" to change your first name, last name and phone number, then press "Save". The phone number needs its country and the digits without the country code.
- The "Email address" card changes the email you sign in with. Type "New email" and "Current password", then press "Change email". The new address is marked Unverified, because Oriel does not send a confirmation email. Use the new address the next time you sign in.
- The "Password" card changes your password. Type "Current password", "New password" and "Confirm new password", then press "Change password". The new password must be at least 8 characters and different from the current one. Changing the password signs your other sessions out.

You cannot change your own role or account status. An Admin does that on the Users page.

### Settings tab

- "Appearance" has a "Dark mode" switch.
- "Date format" lets you choose how dates are shown in Oriel. Each choice shows today's date as an example.
- "Session" shows when you signed in, when your session expires, and your latest sign-in on any device. When the session expires, Oriel asks you to sign in again.

To sign out, open the avatar menu and choose "Logout".

## Who can see what

Oriel shows each person only the pages their role allows. The sidebar on the left lists those pages.

| Sidebar item | Volunteer | Admin | Super Admin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dashboard | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Cats | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Active Applications | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Matching | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Adoptions | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Tasks | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Forms | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Rescue Partners | No | Yes | Yes |
| Fosters | No | Yes | Yes |
| Users | No | Yes | Yes |
| Activity Log | No | Yes | Yes |
| App Settings | No | No | Yes |
| Client Review | No | No | Yes |

The Super Admin can do everything an Admin can do, plus use App Settings and Client Review. The Super Admin role is given to only two accounts and cannot be assigned from the Users page. The role and status of those two accounts cannot be changed from the Users page.

### Other differences between roles

- Only Admins and the Super Admin can see a cat's microchip number. A Volunteer sees "Hidden" in its place, and the number cannot be found through search.
- On the Dashboard, only Admins see the "Invite to Add Cat" button. On the Cats page, only Admins see the "Create Shareable Link" button.
- In global search, a Volunteer finds only the applications and tasks they own. Admins find all of them.
- Who may pick or change an application's Case Owner is a setting in App Settings. By default only Admins can change it.
- If an Admin changes your role while you are signed in, Oriel shows a blue message with your old and new role. Some pages may stop being available, and Oriel moves you to the Dashboard if you were on a page you can no longer open.

If you open a page your role does not allow, Oriel does not show it. Ask an Admin if you think you need access.

## The sidebar

The sidebar lists the pages you can use, with an icon for each. Press the menu button at the top left to collapse the sidebar to icons only, and press it again to open it. On a narrow screen, the sidebar closes by itself after you choose a page. At the bottom of the sidebar, Oriel shows your initials and your role.

For the Super Admin, the sidebar also shows two small counters, "API Requests" and "Database Requests". They are explained in the App Settings guide.

## Global search

The search box is at the top of every staff page. Its hint reads "Search cats, applications, tasks...". Press Ctrl and K together (Cmd and K on a Mac) to jump to it from anywhere.

1. Start typing. Results appear as you type, in groups: "Pages", "Cats", "Applications" and "Tasks". Each group shows at most 5 results.
2. Every word you type must appear in the result. Results that start with your text come first.
3. Choose a result to open it. A page result opens that page, a cat opens its cat page, an application opens its workspace, and a task opens the Tasks page filtered to that task.

What it searches:

- Pages: the names of the sidebar items you can open.
- Cats: name, breed and, for Admins, microchip number.
- Applications: applicant name, email, phone and city.
- Tasks: task title and the linked cat's name.

Search does not cover users, rescue partners or fosters. Use the page search box on the Users, Rescue Partners or Fosters page instead. Oriel loads the search data when you first click the box and refreshes it after a minute, so a record created a moment ago may need a short wait. If a list could not load, the box says which one, and you can click it again to retry.

## Notification bell

The bell icon at the top right shows a red number for notifications you have not read.

1. Press the bell to open the "Notifications" list. Unread items are in bold with a red dot.
2. Press a notification to open the page it refers to. This also marks it as read. If your role cannot open that page, nothing opens.
3. Press "Mark all read" to mark all of them as read. The list shows "You're all caught up." when it is empty.

Notifications are sent for these things:

- A task or an application case is assigned to you or taken away from you.
- Someone mentions you.
- A post-adoption check-in is due for a record you own.
- A cat has been Adoption Ready for a long time or its update is overdue, so the rescuer or foster should be asked for news (Admins and Volunteers).
- A new rescuer application or foster application needs review (Admins).
- A foster has sent a Kitty Profile for a cat (staff).
- A form submission could not be matched to an application, or an escalation needs attention (Admins).

The bell is available only in the staff app. Rescue partners and fosters do not have one.
