# The application page, its tabs and adoption stages

## Who can open an application

Admins and Super Admins can open every application. A Volunteer can open an application only when that Volunteer is its Case Owner. Anyone else sees a message that the application is not assigned to them.

## The header and its buttons

The header shows the applicant name and the current stage. These buttons can appear at the top right.

- "Preview" shows the Pre-Adoption Form answers that were saved with the application, read-only, on one scrolling page. The button is disabled, with an explanation on hover, when no answers were saved. That happens for applications created before Add Application existed or outside Oriel.
- "Email References" appears only while the stage is Active Application. See the section "Sending the second form" below.
- The stage buttons move the application to the next stage. See the section "Adoption stages".

## Adoption stages

An application has one stage at a time. The stages are New Application, Active Application, Approved Application, Rejected Application, Archived Application and Completed Application.

### Buttons in the header

- In New Application, the buttons are "Mark Active" and "Reject". They are disabled until a Case Owner is assigned on the "Overview" tab. Hover over a disabled button to see the reason.
- In Active Application, the buttons are "Approve" and "Reject". They are disabled until a cat is linked to the application on the "Matching" page.
- Each button asks for confirmation. Click "Confirm" to continue or "Cancel" to stop.
- No buttons appear in the other stages.

### What a stage change does to the cats

- "Approve" sets the linked cats to Adopted.
- "Reject" unlinks the cats and sets them back to Adoption Ready. Archiving an application before it is approved does the same.
- Archiving an Approved or Completed application keeps its cats as Adopted.
- An Approved application cannot go back to New Application or Active Application.

### Stage changes that happen automatically

Oriel moves some applications on its own when a form arrives.

- When the Adoption Form arrives (either the UK and other countries version or the UAE version), an application in New Application moves to Active Application.
- When a volunteer issues the final adoption contract through the contract form, an application in New Application or Active Application moves to Approved Application. Oriel also links the cat by microchip number when no cat is linked yet.
- Oriel never moves an application backwards by itself. It never changes an application that is already Approved, Rejected, Archived or Completed, except for the contract move described above.
- Declines, withdrawals and applicants who choose fostering are handled by staff with "Reject" or by archiving the application.

Admins can change the stage to any other value from the adoption page (see the guide section about adoptions).

## Sending the second form

The "Email References" button sends the applicant the second adoption form. It works only for applicants whose country is United Arab Emirates. For every other country the button stays disabled, and the hover text explains that the existing approval flow sends that form itself.

For a UAE applicant, click "Email References". A window opens with these parts.

- "Form": the form to send. The form that fits the applicant country is marked "(suggested)". If you choose the other one, a warning appears.
- "Pre-filled link": the form link with the applicant details already filled in. The arrow icon opens the form so you can check it.
- "Copy link" copies the link so you can paste it into your own email.
- "Open in email" opens your email program with the applicant address, a subject and a message ready. Check the message and send it yourself.
- "Sent before" lists earlier sends with the date, the form and the person.

Oriel cannot send email itself. Either "Copy link" or "Open in email" records that the form was sent. After a first send, the button is named "Resend References". Sending does not change the stage. The button is available to Admins and to the Case Owner.

## Tabs of the application page

The page has ten tabs: "Overview", "Communications", "Tasks", "Screening", "References", "Travel", "Contracts", "Payments", "Post Adoption" and "Activity".

### Overview

The "Overview" tab shows these cards.

- "Applicant Information": name, email, phone, country and city.
- "Case Information": created date, adoption stage, priority, Case Owner, Assigned Volunteer, case health, and the linked cat or cats. The linked cat shows "Not matched yet" until a cat is linked.
- "Application Details": why adopt, adoption motivation, previous cat experience, household, existing pets and work schedule.
- "AI Review": see the section "AI review" below.

The Case Owner can be changed only by the roles that the Super Admin allows in the app settings. By default that is the Admin role. The "Assigned Volunteer" can be changed by anyone who can open the application, and only an active Volunteer can be chosen. Click the pencil icon next to a value to change it.

### Communications

The "Communications" tab is a thread of internal notes about the application. Write in the box "Write internal note... Type @ to mention someone." and click "Send". Typing @ lets you pick a colleague to mention. Web links in a note become clickable. These notes are internal and are not sent to the applicant.

### Tasks

The "Tasks" tab lists the tasks linked to this application. Click "Add Task" to create one that is linked to the application automatically. Admins also get a "My tasks" switch. See the guide section about tasks.

### Screening

The "Screening" tab follows the steps of screening in order. See the section "Screening" below.

### References

The "References" tab has three cards.

- "References": the fields "References submitted" (Yes or No), "Outcome" (Positive, Mixed or Negative) and "Notes". The team fills these in.
- "Referees": three cards, "Referee 1", "Referee 2" and "Referee 3". Each shows a name, a phone number and an email. Click the pencil icon, enter "Name", "Phone" and "Email", and click "Save". Phone numbers and emails are links you can click to call or write. The names normally come from the Adoption Form, and you can type them yourself.
- "Reference checks": one card for each referee who has answered the Reference Check form, with all their answers and a signature link or the signed name. The card shows a red "Knows of criminal history" label and an alert when the referee answered Yes to the question about criminal history. Oriel does not notify anyone about this, so check the card. Before any referee has answered, the card says that no referee has answered yet.

When the first Reference Check answer arrives, "References submitted" is set to Yes.

### Travel

The "Travel" tab holds the travel plan for the cat. See the section "Travel" below.

### Contracts

The "Contracts" tab has two cards.

- "Contract status" shows three items with a Yes or No: "Draft Contract Generated", "Final Contract Sent" and "Signed Contract Received".
- "Contract files" lists the uploaded contract files. Click "Upload files" to add files and choose a type for each: "Draft contract", "Final contract (sent)", "Signed contract" or "Other".

Uploading a file of the first three types sets the matching status to Yes. Deleting a file does not change a status. When files of more than one type exist, chips such as "All" let you filter the list.

### Payments

The "Payments" tab has "Payment status" and "Payment date". The status can be Pending, Paid, Not Sent or Refunded. Payments are marked manually in Oriel. When you choose Paid and no date is set, Oriel fills in today's date. Oriel never clears the date by itself. To clear it, edit "Payment date" and leave it empty.

### Post Adoption

The "Post Adoption" tab is described in the guide section about adoptions and post-adoption follow-up.

### Activity

The "Activity" tab lists the history of changes made to the application, with who made each change and when.

## Screening

The "Screening" tab shows the steps in this order: Call 1, Home video, Call 2, and then any extra calls. Admins and the Case Owner can use this tab.

### Call 1

Call 1 has the fields "Date", "Completed" (Yes or No), "Sentiment" (Positive, Neutral or Concern) and "Summary". Below them is the "Transcript" area.

### Home video

The "Home video" step has "Video submitted" (Yes or No), "Video approved" (Yes, No or Needs Clarification) and "Review notes". Click "Upload video" to add the video file. The list of video files shows who uploaded each file and when. Each file can be deleted after a confirmation.

### Call 2

Call 2 has the fields "Date" and "Summary", and the same "Transcript" area as Call 1. It also has the field "Call 2 required". The rest of the Call 2 step is greyed out until "Call 2 required" is set to Yes. The field "Call 2 required" itself can always be changed.

### Extra calls

Click "Add call" at the bottom of the tab to add another call. The first one is Call 3, the next is Call 4, and so on, up to 20 extra calls. An extra call has a "Date", a "Summary", a transcript area and the AI review button, like Call 2. Click "Delete call" on an extra call to remove it. The confirmation says that this deletes the date, the summary and every transcript of that call, and that it cannot be undone. Past AI reviews of the call stay in the history.

### Transcripts

Each call has a "Transcript" area with these buttons.

- "Upload file" adds a transcript file. Allowed types are text, subtitle (VTT and SRT), Word and PDF files.
- "Paste text" opens a window where you paste the transcript text.
- "Review call with AI" starts the AI review of the call.

Click a pasted transcript to open its full text in a window. That window has "Copy text" and "Close". Each transcript can be deleted after a confirmation. Before any transcript exists, the area says "No transcript yet. Upload the file from the call tool, or paste the text."

## AI review

Oriel can ask an AI to review an application. The AI only makes a draft. A person decides whether to keep it.

### Reviewing the form

On the "Overview" tab, click "Review form with AI" in the "AI Review" card. The AI reads the application answers and suggests a summary, concerns, missing information, suggested questions, a recommendation and a risk score.

### Reviewing a call

On the "Screening" tab, click "Review call with AI" in a call. The button works only when the call has a transcript. The AI reads the transcript and suggests a call summary, the other review texts, a recommendation, a risk score and a suggested next action. For Call 1 it also suggests the sentiment and whether Call 2 is required.

### Checking the draft

- After a review runs, a message appears: "New AI draft", with the kind of review and the date. Click "Review draft".
- A window shows a table with three columns: "Field", "Current value" and "AI suggests".
- Click "Accept draft" to save every suggested value into the application. The change is logged in the "Activity" tab. Values cannot be accepted one by one.
- Click "Discard draft" to throw the draft away. Nothing changes.
- Click "Close" to leave the draft waiting for later.

### The AI Review card

The "AI Review" card on the "Overview" tab shows the accepted values: "Recommendation" (Needs Review, Proceed or High Risk), "Risk score" out of 100, "Summary", "Review", "Concerns", "Missing information", "Suggested next action" and "Suggested questions". A higher risk score means a riskier application. The line at the bottom says which review the values came from and who accepted it. The link "AI history" opens the earlier runs with what the AI suggested and what it was given.

Treat the AI result as a help. Check it against the application before acting on it. If a "Mock AI" label appears on the card, the text is sample text and not a real review. If the AI is not set up, a message says so, and transcripts can still be added.

## Travel

The "Travel" tab is available to Admins and the Case Owner. An application has at most one travel record.

- Before a record exists, the tab shows "Start travel" with an optional "Travel date (optional)". Starting creates the record with the status Planning, linked to the application and its cat.
- The record has these fields: "Status" (Awaiting Information, Planning, Ready, In Transit, Completed or Cancelled), the cat, "Travel date", "Arrival date", "Flight number", "Crate size" (Small, Medium, Large or XL), "Crate weight" with a kg or lb choice, "Departure airport", "Arrival airport" and "Travel notes".
- Each field is saved when you change it.
- The crate weight must be above zero. A weight without a unit is saved in kg. The arrival date cannot be before the travel date.
- Changes are written to the activity log. Nobody is notified about travel changes.

The Travel tab on the cat page shows the same travel record of the application the cat is matched to. An edit on either page appears on the other. A travel record can only be started from the application page.
