# Fosters: Applications, Approval and the Foster Portal

## What a foster is

A foster is a person who looks after a cat at home for IBKT. A foster does not use the staff app. After IBKT approves the application, the foster gets their own sign-in and a small portal. In the portal the foster sees the cats placed with them and sends a Kitty Profile for each cat. Staff then review the profile and add it to the cat.

## The Fosters page

Admins and the Super Admin open the page from the sidebar item "Fosters". Volunteers cannot see it. The page has two tabs:

- "Applications": the foster applications. This tab opens first and shows a count of applications waiting for review.
- "Fosters": the foster accounts.

The button "Add Foster" at the top right adds a foster directly.

## How someone becomes a foster

1. The person fills in the foster application form. Staff send them the link from the "Forms" page, where the form is listed as "Foster Application". Press "Copy link" next to it to copy the link, or "Open" to see the form.
2. Oriel emails the person to say the application was received. Every active Admin gets a notification on the bell.
3. An Admin reviews the application in the "Applications" tab and approves or rejects it.
4. Oriel emails the person either way. An approved person receives a link to choose a password.
5. The foster signs in on the page "Foster Sign In".

## The foster application form

The form is called "Become a Foster". It needs no sign-in. It is a copy of the IBKT Foster Compliance and Safeguarding Form. The person moves through the steps with "Next" and "Back".

- "About you": name, phone, email and home address.
- "Foster commitment": statements the person must answer Yes to in order to apply. It also asks whether any resident pets are vaccinated and neutered or spayed.
- "Responsibility": statements the person must answer Yes to.
- "Emergency contact": name, relationship, phone, email and address of an emergency contact.
- "References": three references, each with name, relationship, phone and email.
- "Safeguarding": a question about any disagreement with a rescue organisation, or rehoming an animal independently, with a box for details if the answer is Yes.
- "Declaration": a final Yes statement and a typed full name that acts as the signature.

The last button is "Submit Application". The person then sees "Application Sent" and is told they will get an email once a decision is made. Oriel accepts at most a few applications per email address in an hour.

A person who was rejected can apply again. A person who has a Pending application or an approved account cannot apply again with the same email.

## Reviewing applications

In the "Applications" tab, use the chips "Pending", "Approved", "Rejected" and "All" to filter. Each chip shows a count, and "Pending" is selected first. The table has the columns "Name", "Email", "Sent", "Status" and "Actions".

- "View" opens the full application, read-only, step by step. It also shows who approved or rejected it. Two warnings appear at the top when the person answered No to the resident pets question, or reported a disagreement with a rescue organisation or independent rehoming. Read these before you decide.
- "Approve" (Pending only) opens the question to approve the application. Confirming creates the foster record and a sign-in, marks the foster agreement as signed with today's date, and emails the person a link, valid for 7 days, to choose a password.
- "Reject" (Pending only) opens the question to reject the application. Type a "Reason", which is required and is included in the email to the applicant. The person can apply again later.

A green message tells you whether the email went out. If the email was not sent, use "Resend sign-in link" on the foster's row in the "Fosters" tab.

If an approval stops half way, approve again to finish it.

## The Fosters tab

The "Fosters" tab lists the foster accounts in the same way as the Users page, without a "Role" column. Use the chips "All", "Active", "Pending", "Blocked", "Suspended" and "Archived", and the box "Search by name or email". The three-dot menu in each row offers the same actions as the Users page (see the Users guide), plus "Resend sign-in link". That action emails a fresh link, valid for 7 days, to choose a password.

To add a foster without the application form:

1. Press "Add Foster".
2. Fill in "First Name", "Last Name" and "Email" (required), and optionally "Country", "City", "Address" and "Notes".
3. Press "Add Foster".

The foster is created as Active straight away and is emailed a link, valid for 7 days, to choose a password. No agreement answers are recorded for them.

## Placing a cat with a foster

A cat shows a foster in the "Foster" row on the Overview tab of the cat's page. Any staff member who can open the cat page (Admin, Super Admin or Volunteer) can set it.

1. Open the cat and go to the Overview tab.
2. Hover over the "Foster" row and press the pencil button.
3. Choose a foster from the list, which shows the name and city of each active foster, or choose none.
4. Save with the tick.

Only an active foster can be chosen. Clearing the foster is always allowed. One foster can have several cats. The foster is not notified when a cat is placed with them, so tell them yourself. The cat then appears in their portal.

## The foster portal

The foster signs in on "Foster Sign In" with email and password. A new foster follows the link in the approval email to the page "Choose Your Password", types "Password" and "Confirm Password" (at least 8 characters), presses "Set Password", and then signs in.

The portal has a plain top bar with the link "My Foster Cats", the foster's name and "Sign out". It has no sidebar, search or bell.

### My Foster Cats

The page shows a welcome line and a card for each cat placed with the foster. If there are none, it says "IBKT has not placed a cat with you yet. When it does, the cat will be listed here." Each card shows the cat's name, a status tag and one button.

| Status tag | Meaning | Button |
|---|---|---|
| No profile sent yet | The foster has not sent a profile. | "Send Kitty Profile" |
| Waiting for review | A profile was sent and IBKT has not dealt with it. It cannot be changed now. | "Waiting for review" (disabled) |
| Added to the cat | IBKT used the profile on the cat. | "Send an updated profile" |
| Needs changes | IBKT sent the profile back. The reason is shown on the card. | "Send a new profile" |

The page also shows the date the profile was sent and the support email address for questions.

### Sending a Kitty Profile

The page "Kitty Profile for" the cat's name says "Tell us about the cat so we can find it the right home." It has these steps: "Household", "Medical history", "Litterbox", "Behaviour", "Profile" and "Photos and videos".

1. Answer the questions on each step and press "Next". Oriel highlights any answer that needs fixing.
2. On the "Photos and videos" step, add the files. Each file is uploaded as soon as you choose it, and you can leave the page and come back later. The files stay until the profile is sent. You can remove your own files before sending.
3. Press "Send Profile".

The profile cannot be sent until there are at least 6 photos and 3 videos. The page lists what is still missing. The pet passport and the vet report are asked for but not required. The limits are up to 10 photos of 10 MB each, 5 videos of 20 MB each, 1 passport of 10 MB, 3 vet reports of 10 MB each, and 25 files in total.

The typed answers are kept on the page only until the profile is sent. If the foster leaves the page before sending, the answers are lost, but the uploaded files remain.

After sending, the foster returns to "My Foster Cats" with a green message. Every active staff account gets a bell notification that links to the cat. A cat can have only one profile waiting for review at a time.

## Reviewing a Kitty Profile (staff)

When a foster has sent a profile, the Overview tab of the cat's page shows a card called "Foster submissions" at the top. Each row shows the status (Pending, Applied or Sent back), the foster, the date and who dealt with it. Any staff role that can open the cat can use it.

1. Press "Review" on a Pending row. The profile opens read-only, with the answers step by step and the files by kind. Files of a waiting profile have links you can open.
2. Press "Apply to the cat" to use the profile, or "Send back" to return it.

When you press "Apply to the cat", a table shows each cat field the answers speak about, the current value, and the proposed "Change to" value. Tick the fields you want to change and edit any proposed value. A field is ticked by default only when the cat has no answer for it yet. Long texts such as the personality summary and notes are added after the text that is already there. A text field is limited to 2000 characters, and the dialog shows a counter. Apply refuses a longer text.

A tick box lets you also move the foster's files onto the cat: photos to Photos, videos to Videos, the passport to Passport File, and the vet report to Medical documents. Press "Apply" to save. The profile becomes Applied, and the foster sees "Added to the cat".

When you press "Send back", type a "Reason" (required). The foster sees the reason in their portal, can change the profile and files, and send it again. Nothing on the cat changes. The foster is not emailed. They see the result in the portal.

Use "View" on a row that is already Applied or Sent back to read it again.
