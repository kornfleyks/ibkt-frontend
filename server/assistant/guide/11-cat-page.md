# Cat page

Click a cat on the Cats list to open its page. Admins and Volunteers can open it. This guide explains the header, each tab, changing a cat's status, the Adoption Ready check, the 3-month update check and the details that only Admins can see.

## Header

The top of the page shows the cat's name, its status, and two labels for the FeLV and FIV results. It also shows the rescuer, the age and the date the cat was created.

Below the header is a row of tabs: "Overview", "Medical", "Documents", "Advert", "Communications", "Tasks", "Travel", "Post Adoption" and "Activity". The tab you open is kept in the page address.

## Changing a cat's status

The status can be changed in two places: next to the cat's name in the header, and in the "Status" row of the "Rescue Information" card on the Overview tab. Move the pointer over the status and click the pencil icon. Pick the new status, then click the tick to save or the cross to cancel.

The statuses are Awaiting Profile, Intake, Awaiting Passport, Awaiting Media, Adoption Ready, Reserved, Adopted, Fostered and Archived.

Matching changes the status too. Matching a cat sets it to Reserved. Removing a match sets it back to Adoption Ready.

### Adoption Ready check

Oriel does not let you set a cat to Adoption Ready, Reserved or Adopted until the cat's details are complete. If something is missing, a window opens with the title "[Cat name] is not ready to be set to [status]". It lists what is still missing, grouped as Profile, Medical, Passport and Media.

A cat is ready when all of these are filled in.

- Profile: gender, age, breed, colour, energy level, personality summary, and the answers for child friendly, cat friendly and dog friendly. The answers "Unknown" and "N/A" do not count.
- Medical: vaccinated (Yes or Planned), sterilised (Yes or Planned), FeLV tested (Negative or Positive), FIV tested (Negative or Positive), a medical summary and a microchip number. The answers "Not Tested Yet" and "Pending" do not count.
- Passport: "Passport marked complete" is switched on and a passport file is uploaded.
- Media: at least 6 photos and at least 3 videos.

A Volunteer cannot continue. The window only has a "Close" button. Add the missing details, then try again.

An Admin can set the status anyway. The window asks for a "Reason" (up to 500 characters). The "Set anyway" button saves the status, and the reason is saved in the Activity Log with the list of what was missing.

The check does not apply to a cat that is already Adoption Ready, Reserved or Adopted. A cat that returns to Adoption Ready after a match falls through is not checked again.

## Overview tab

The Overview tab has these parts.

- "Foster submissions" appears only when a foster has sent a Kitty Profile for the cat. Each line shows who sent it and when. Click "Review" for a new profile, or "View" for one that was already handled. An Admin or Volunteer can then click "Apply to the cat" to copy the foster's answers into the cat, or "Send back" to return the profile to the foster with a reason. In the apply window, tick the fields to change, edit any value, and choose whether to move the foster's files to the cat. Nothing on the cat changes when a profile is sent back.
- "Basic Information" shows "Name", "Gender", "Age", "Bonded With", "Breed" and "Colour". Move the pointer over a row and click the pencil icon to edit it. "Bonded With" lists the cats that are bonded to this cat. Click the link icon to bond another cat. A bonded group can have only a limited number of cats, which is set in App Settings.
- "Rescue Information" shows "Rescuer", "Foster", "Status" and "Foster contact". The rescuer, the foster and the status can be edited with the pencil icon.
- "Adoption readiness" lists what is missing before the cat can be set to Adoption Ready, or says that the cat is ready.
- "Update check" shows the 3-month update check, described below.

## Medical tab

The "Medical Status" card shows "Vaccinated", "Sterilized", "Passport" (Yes or No, from the uploaded passport file) and "Medication Required". The "Additional Information" card shows "Microchip", "FeLV Status" and "FIV Status". Move the pointer over a row and click the pencil icon to edit it.

### Passport section

The "Cat Passport" card is at the bottom of the Medical tab. Any staff member can edit it. It holds these items.

- Six dates: "Date of birth", "Microchip implant date", "Last dewormed", "First Tricat", "Booster Tricat" and "Rabies vaccination". Each date is saved as soon as you change it. Clear a date to remove it.
- "Passport file" shows how many files are uploaded. Upload the scanned passport on the Documents tab.
- "Passport marked complete" is a switch. Switch it on when the dates and the scanned passport are complete. A cat cannot be set to Adoption Ready until it is on. Oriel does not check that the dates are filled in.

## Documents tab

The Documents tab has four sections: "Passport", "Medical Documents", "Photos" and "Videos". Each section has an "Upload" button. You can pick several files at once. A file can be at most 20 MB. Passport and Medical Documents show Yes or No for whether a file exists.

Click a file name to open it in a new browser tab. Move the pointer over a file and click the bin icon to delete it. Oriel asks "Delete file?" and the deletion cannot be undone.

## Advert tab

The Advert tab shows the "Draft advert" for the cat. The AI writes the first draft when the cat is added. A draft is never published.

- The draft text appears with a line that says who wrote it and when. A draft written by the AI says "Written by the AI". A draft that staff changed says "Edited by" followed by the name.
- "Edit" opens the text so that you can change it. Click "Save" to keep your changes or "Cancel" to leave. Your edit is saved as a new version, and the AI's text stays in the history.
- "Write it yourself" appears instead of "Edit" when there is no draft yet.
- "Write a draft" or "Write a new draft" asks the AI to write another draft. This button is disabled when draft adverts are switched off. In that case the tab says that draft adverts are not switched on. Editing still works.
- If the last attempt failed, the tab shows a warning with the reason.
- "Approve" appears only for Admins and the Super Admin, next to the other buttons, when the advert is a draft. Oriel asks "Approve this advert?" and says that the advert will be locked. Click "Approve and lock" to confirm. After that the tab is called "Advert" and shows "Approved by" followed by the name and the time. An approved advert cannot be edited or replaced, and nobody can unlock it in Oriel, so check the text before approving. The "Edit", "Write a draft" and "Write a new draft" buttons are no longer shown.

The AI uses only the cat's description and medical details. It does not use contact details, the location, the microchip number or photos. Rejecting a draft is not available yet.

## Communications tab

The Communications tab holds internal notes about the cat. Type a note in the box and click "Send". Type "@" to mention a colleague. The notes are for staff only.

## Tasks tab

The Tasks tab lists the tasks linked to this cat. Click "Add Task" to create one. The form asks for a "Title" and an "Owner" (both required), and also "Description", "Status", "Priority", "Due Date" and "Waiting Reason". The "Waiting Reason" is needed when the status is Waiting. Admins see all tasks of the cat. A Volunteer sees only the tasks assigned to that Volunteer. Admins also have a "My tasks" switch.

## Travel tab

The Travel tab shows the travel record of the application that the cat is matched to. It is the same record as on the application's Travel tab. Only Admins and the Case Owner of that application can see the details. Other people see a message that says so.

- If the cat is not matched to an application, the tab says there is no travel record.
- If the application has no travel record yet, the tab says so and shows an "Open the application" button. Start the record on the application's Travel tab.
- Otherwise the tab shows the application name as a link and the travel fields, which you can edit here.

## Post Adoption tab

The Post Adoption tab shows the post-adoption record of the application that the cat is matched to. It is the same tab as on the application and has the same rules. If the cat is not matched to an application, the tab says there is no post-adoption record. Otherwise it shows the application name as a link and the post-adoption details.

## Activity tab

The Activity tab lists the changes made to the cat, such as edits, status changes and overrides of the Adoption Ready check. Admins can see the whole log of the app on the "Activity Log" page.

## Update check (every 3 months)

A cat that waits for a home needs regular news from its rescuer or foster. The "Update check" card on the Overview tab tracks this.

- The check applies only while the cat is Adoption Ready. For other statuses the card says that updates are asked for only while a cat is Adoption Ready and waiting for a home.
- The card shows "Update status", "Adoption Ready since", "Next update due", "Update requested on" and "Last update received" with the name of the person and an optional "Note".
- The update status is Not Due, Requested, Overdue or Received.
- Three months after the cat became Adoption Ready, Oriel sets the status to Requested. Every active Admin and Volunteer gets a notification in the bell that links to the cat. They should ask the rescuer or the foster for news.
- If nobody answers for 14 days, the status becomes Overdue and everyone is notified again.
- Click "Update received" when you have news. A window asks for an optional "Note". Click "Record update". This closes the request, sets the status to Received and restarts the 3 months.
- Editing the cat does not count as an update. Only "Update received" does.
- A cat that was already Adoption Ready when the check started counts from the first day the check saw it.

## Who can see what

- The microchip number is visible to Admins and the Super Admin only. A Volunteer sees "Hidden (only Admins can see it)" on the Medical tab and has no edit button. The Cats list search and the header search do not show or match the number for a Volunteer. The Activity Log also hides the old and new values.
- Any staff member can type a microchip number when adding a cat, but a Volunteer cannot see it afterwards.
- A cat still counts as having a microchip number for the Adoption Ready check, even when the person who asks cannot see it.
- Only Admins can override the Adoption Ready check.
- Only Admins can use "Create Shareable Link" on the Cats list.
- The Travel and Post Adoption tabs show details only to Admins and the Case Owner of the matched application.
