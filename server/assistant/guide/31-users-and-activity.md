# Users, Recent Activity and the Activity Log

## The Users page

The "Users" page lists the staff accounts of Oriel. Admins and the Super Admin can open it from the sidebar item "Users". Volunteers cannot see it.

The page lists Admins, Volunteers and Super Admins. Rescue partners and fosters are not listed here. They are managed on the "Rescue Partners" and "Fosters" pages.

The subtitle under the page title shows how many accounts are waiting for approval, for example "2 account(s) awaiting approval".

### Finding an account

- Use the tabs "All", "Active", "Pending", "Blocked", "Suspended" and "Archived". Each tab shows how many accounts it holds.
- Type in "Search by name or email" to narrow the list.

### What the table shows

The columns are "Name", "Email", "Role", "Status", "Last login", "Created", "Password" and "Actions". "Last login" shows how long ago the person signed in, or "Never".

- To correct a name or an email, hover over it and press the pencil button, then save. The copy button next to each one copies the text.
- "Role" is a drop-down list with Volunteer and Admin. Choosing another role opens the question "Change role?". Press "Change role" to confirm. The new role applies on the person's next action, and the person sees a message that their role changed. The two Super Admin accounts show a lock and their role cannot be changed here.
- "Password" has a "Set Password" button. Press it, type "New Password" and "Confirm" (at least 8 characters) and save. Use this when someone is locked out and cannot use the reset email.

## Adding a user

1. Press "Add User" at the top right.
2. Fill in "First Name", "Last Name", "Email" and "Role" (Volunteer or Admin).
3. Oriel fills in a random "Password". Press the copy button to copy it, or type your own (at least 8 characters).
4. Press "Add User".

The account is created as Active straight away, with no approval step. Oriel does not email the password. Copy it and give it to the new person yourself. They can change it later on the "My Account" page.

## Approving or rejecting a new sign-up

People who use "Create an account" on the sign-in page appear in the "Pending" tab with the role Volunteer. They cannot sign in until an Admin acts.

1. Open the "Pending" tab.
2. Press the three dots in the "Actions" column of the row.
3. Choose "Approve" to let the person sign in, or "Reject" to refuse. Rejecting sets the account to Archived.
4. Press the confirm button in the question that opens.

Approving gives the person Volunteer access to the staff app, so check who they are before you approve.

## Account statuses and actions

Every account has one status. The three dots in the "Actions" column open a menu with "View details", "Send Test Email" and the actions for the current status.

| Status | Meaning | Actions offered |
|---|---|---|
| Pending | Waiting for approval. Cannot sign in. | "Approve", "Reject" |
| Active | Can sign in. | "Block", "Suspend", "Archive" |
| Blocked | Temporary lock. Signed out straight away and cannot sign in. Open cases and tasks stay with the person. | "Unblock", "Suspend", "Archive" |
| Suspended | Cannot sign in. Signed out on their next action. | "Reactivate", "Archive" |
| Archived | Kept only for the record. Cannot sign in. | "Restore" |

Notes about these actions:

- Each action opens a question that names the person and the new status. Nothing changes until you press the confirm button.
- "Suspend" and "Archive" hand the person's open cases and tasks to the Admin who confirms. The question lists how many cases and tasks will move. Moving them means the work is not left with someone who cannot sign in. "Block" does not move any work.
- "Restore" lets you choose "Restore as Active" or "Restore as Pending (needs approval again)".
- You cannot block, suspend or archive your own account.
- The two Super Admin accounts have no status actions.
- "View details" opens a side panel with the email, role, status, last login, and the person's open cases and open tasks.
- "Send Test Email" sends a test email to the person, to check that their address works.
- "Delete" removes an account permanently, and it cannot be undone. It appears only for one Super Admin account, and never for the account itself. Archive an account instead if you only want to stop access.

## Recent Activity on the Dashboard

The Dashboard has a "Recent Activity" panel. It lists the five latest work events, newest first, each with an icon, a short description and how long ago it happened.

- It shows only work events: something created, updated, commented on, approved or rejected. Sign-ins, sign-outs, registrations and password resets are not shown.
- Press an item to open the cat, application, task list or user it refers to. An item without a link is shown as plain text.
- You see only events for the pages your role can open. A Volunteer sees events about cats, applications and tasks. Admins also see events about other things.
- Admins see a "View all" button that opens the "Activity Log".
- The panel says "No recent activity." when there is nothing to show.

## The Activity Log page

The "Activity Log" page shows everything that has happened across the app. Admins and the Super Admin can open it from the sidebar item "Activity Log". The page title has the subtitle "Everything that's happened across the app".

- Each entry has a coloured icon for its type, a description, a label for the area it belongs to, the date and time, and the name of the person who did it. The name shows "Unknown" when the person is not known.
- The entry types are Created, Updated, Commented, Login, Logout, Register, Password Reset, Approved and Rejected.
- A "Register" entry is a link. Press it to go to the Users page and approve the new account.
- To find entries, type in the search box "Search activity by person, item, board, or action...". It matches the description, the person, the area, the item name and the type. The list says "No activity matches your search." when nothing matches.
- The log is read-only. You cannot edit or delete entries.

Each cat and application page also has its own "Activity" tab, which shows only the history of that one record.
