# Rescue Partners: Sign-Up, Agreement and the Rescuer Portal

## What a rescue partner is

A rescue partner is someone outside IBKT who rescues cats and wants IBKT to help rehome them. In the app, the sign-in pages and the role are still called "Rescuer", while the staff page is called "Rescue Partners". A rescue partner does not use the staff app. After signing up, they use a small portal where they add cats for IBKT to review.

## The Rescue Partners page

Admins and the Super Admin open the page from the sidebar item "Rescue Partners". Volunteers cannot see it. The page has three tabs, each with a count:

- "Wix requests": people who filled in the rescuer form on the IBKT website. This is the tab that opens first.
- "Applications": rescuer sign-up forms that need an Admin to review them.
- "Rescue Partners": the rescue partner accounts.

The button "Add Rescue Partner" at the top right adds a rescue partner directly.

## How someone becomes a rescue partner

The normal path has four steps.

1. The person fills in the rescuer form on the IBKT website. The request appears in the "Wix requests" tab with the status "Applied". The Oriel team gets in touch with the person, for example for a call.
2. An Admin decides to accept the person and presses "Approve and send agreement". Oriel emails the person a link to read and sign the Rescuer Agreement.
3. The person signs the agreement. Oriel then sends them a link to the sign-up form, and sends them a copy of the signed agreement.
4. The person fills in the sign-up form and chooses a password. Oriel creates their account and signs them in to the rescuer portal straight away. No Admin approval is needed at this step.

The "Wix requests" tab shows the line "Applied, Approved, Signed. Signed partners can add cats."

## The Wix requests tab

Use the filter chips "All", "Applied", "Approved" and "Signed" to narrow the list. Each chip shows a count. A request that has not been approved yet is highlighted in a pale red.

The table has the columns "Rescue partner" (name and email), "Status", "Cats" (how many cats the person wants to rehome), "Next step" and "Action".

| Status tag | What it means | Button in "Action" |
|---|---|---|
| Applied | Waiting for an Admin decision. | "Approve and send agreement" |
| Approved | The agreement email was sent and is not signed yet. | "Resend agreement" |
| Link expired | The agreement link ran out before it was signed. | "Resend agreement" |
| Signed | The agreement is signed. The tag stays Signed after the person has signed up. | "Resend sign-up link" (until they have signed up) |

The "Next step" column explains the situation in words, for example when the agreement was sent and until when the link works, or that the person "Signed up and can add cats". If an email could not be sent, the column shows a red note.

To act on a request:

1. Press the button in the "Action" column. A question opens that names the action and says which email will go out.
2. Press "Send email" to confirm, or "Cancel".
3. A green message at the top says the email was sent. If it was not sent, the message says so, and you can press the button again.

Sending the agreement again makes a new link, and the older link stops working. A request without an email address has no button.

## The agreement page

The person opens the link from the email and sees the page "Your Rescuer Agreement".

- "In short" lists the main points. The person ticks the points they agree to and must tick at least one.
- "Read the full agreement" opens the full document in a new tab. The page also shows its version.
- The person types their full name under "Type your full name to sign" and presses "Sign the agreement".
- The link works once. If it is used up or expired, the page says so, and an Admin sends a new one.

After signing, the page opens the sign-up form. The person also receives an email with the signed copy and the sign-up link, and Oriel keeps a record of the version they signed.

## The sign-up form

The form is called "Become a Rescuer". It opens only from the sign-up link in the email. Opening the plain address without that link shows a message to contact IBKT. The email address comes from the agreement and cannot be changed.

The form has four steps, "About you", "Your rescue work", "Agreement" and "Declaration". The person moves with "Next" and "Back".

- "About you": first name, last name, email, phone, WhatsApp number (optional), country, city and preferred contact method (Email, WhatsApp or Phone).
- "Your rescue work": a description of the cats they rescue, the name of their group if any, how they heard about IBKT, and anything else.
- "Agreement": a list of statements. The person must answer Yes to each one.
- "Declaration": a final Yes statement and a typed full name that acts as the signature. On this last step the person also chooses a password and confirms it (at least 8 characters).

The last button is "Submit and Sign In". After it, Oriel creates the account and opens the rescuer portal.

If Oriel cannot create the account automatically, for example because the email already has an account, the person sees "Application Sent" and is told an Admin will review the application. That application then needs an Admin, as described in the next section.

## The Applications tab

This tab lists the sign-up forms, with the columns "Name", "Email", "Sent", "Status" and "Actions". Use the chips "Pending", "Approved", "Rejected" and "All" to filter. The chip "Pending" is selected first.

In most cases this tab has nothing pending, because sign-ups are approved automatically. A Pending application is one where the account could not be created.

- "View" opens the answers the applicant gave, read-only.
- "Approve" (Pending only) opens the question "Approve <name>?". Confirming creates the rescue partner record and sign-in, and emails the person a link, valid for 7 days, to choose a password.
- "Reject" (Pending only) opens the question "Reject <name>?". Type a "Reason" (required, at most 1000 characters). The applicant is emailed the reason and can apply again later.

A green message tells you whether the email went out. If it did not, use "Resend sign-in link" on the person's row in the "Rescue Partners" tab.

## The Rescue Partners tab and adding a partner directly

The "Rescue Partners" tab lists the accounts, in the same way as the Users page but without a "Role" column. Use the chips "All", "Active", "Pending", "Blocked", "Suspended" and "Archived", and the box "Search by name or email". The three-dot menu in each row offers the same actions as the Users page (see the Users guide), plus "Resend sign-in link". That action emails a fresh link, valid for 7 days, to choose a password. Use it when the first link was lost or expired.

To add someone without the website form and agreement:

1. Press "Add Rescue Partner".
2. Fill in "First Name", "Last Name" and "Email" (required), and optionally phone, "WhatsApp number", "Preferred contact method", "Country", "City" and "Notes".
3. Press "Add Rescuer".

The partner is created as Active straight away and is emailed a link, valid for 7 days, to choose a password. Because they did not fill in the form, no agreement answers are recorded for them.

## The rescuer portal

Rescue partners sign in on the page "Rescuer Sign In" with their email and password. A new partner who got a "Choose Your Password" link types "Password" and "Confirm Password" (at least 8 characters), presses "Set Password", and then signs in.

The portal has a plain top bar with the links "My Cats" and "Add Cat", the partner's name, and "Sign out". It has no sidebar, search or bell.

### My Cats

The "My Cats" page lists the cats the partner added. Its subtitle says "The cats you have added. To change anything, contact IBKT." Each card shows the cat's name, its status, the date added, and the number of photos, videos and documents. The page says "You have not added any cats yet." when it is empty. A partner cannot edit a cat after adding it. They contact IBKT, using the support address shown at the bottom of the page.

The page has three buttons: "Add Cat", "Invite to Add Cat" and "Create Shareable Link".

### Adding a cat

1. Press "Add Cat". The page "Add Cat" says "Tell us about a cat you want to rehome. IBKT will review it."
2. Fill in the form. Only "Name" is required. The sections are "About the cat" (name, gender, age in years, breed, colour), "Personality" (personality, energy level, lap cat, indoor only, child friendly, cat friendly, dog friendly, special notes), "Health" (medical summary, vaccinated, neutered or spayed, FeLV status, FIV status, medication required, microchip number) and "Where the cat is now" (foster contact and foster location).
3. Add photos, videos and medical documents if you have them. Each kind allows up to 5 files, photos and documents up to 10 MB each and videos up to 20 MB each. A file that is the wrong type or too large is flagged, and you must fix it first.
4. Press "Add Cat".

Oriel creates the cat with the status Intake and links it to the partner. The partner cannot choose another status or another owner. IBKT staff then review the cat. Oriel returns to "My Cats" with a green message.

To add several bonded cats at once, press "Add Another Cat". Each cat gets its own tab and needs its own details, and the cats are linked together as a bonded group. A limit on the group size applies, and the page tells you when it is reached. The final button then reads "Add 2 Cats" (or the number of cats). Press the small cross on a tab to remove that cat.

If a file fails to upload, the cat is still added. The green message lists the files that failed, and the partner contacts IBKT to add them.

### Inviting someone else to add a cat

A rescue partner can let another person, such as a foster, fill in the Add Cat form without an account.

- "Invite to Add Cat" asks for the person's full name and email and whether they are a foster or a rescuer, then press "Send Invitation". Oriel emails them a link. The link works for 3 days and for one cat only.
- "Create Shareable Link" asks "Who is the link for?" with the choices "A foster (the form asks which foster)" and "A rescuer". Press "Create Link". Oriel shows the link and the date it expires. Press "Copy Link" and send it yourself. The link works for one cat only.

The person opens the link on the page "Add a Cat" and does not need to sign in. If the link is for a foster, the form also asks "Who is fostering this cat?". The person types "Foster's email" and presses "Check". If the foster is found and active, the cat is linked to them. If the email is not found, the person fills in the foster's first name, surname and phone. If a foster record is found but is not active, the person is told to contact IBKT. After sending, the page says "Thank you" and that IBKT will review the cat. A used or expired link shows "This link has expired or has already been used. Ask for a new one."

Staff can also make these links. Admins see "Invite to Add Cat" on the Dashboard and "Create Shareable Link" on the "Cats" page.
