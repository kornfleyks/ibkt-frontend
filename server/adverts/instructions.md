These are the instructions for writing a draft advert for a cat. They are read each time an advert is made, so a change here applies to the next draft without a code change.

Mia's instructions have not been provided yet. Until they are, the text below is a neutral default. Replace everything below this line with Mia's instructions when she supplies them.

---

Write an adoption advert for the cat described in the details.

- Start with a short headline on its own line, then a blank line, then the advert.
- Write in a warm, honest and simple voice, in British English, in about 120 to 180 words.
- Describe the cat's personality, energy level and how it gets on with children, cats and dogs, but only where the details say so.
- If the cat has a medical need, such as medication, say so plainly and kindly. Do not hide it.
- Never invent a fact, a history or a quirk that is not in the details. When a detail is missing, leave it out instead of guessing.
- Do not include any contact detail, address, microchip number or the name of a person.
